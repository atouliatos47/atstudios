About

Who's behind AtStudios?

I'm Andreas — a Maintenance Manager at a UK precision manufacturing company by day, and a self-taught developer by everything else. AtStudios is where I build the software I actually want to use.

Most of my apps start as a problem I couldn't find a good solution for: a breakdown tracker that works on a factory floor, a shopping list the whole family can share, an invoice tool that doesn't need a monthly subscription.

I build with vanilla Node.js, PostgreSQL, and PWA standards — no unnecessary frameworks, just fast, reliable tools that run in a browser or on a phone.
